The craft

Our story

It started with a dropped burger.

Halfway down, it looked perfect — every layer separated, floating, exactly where it belonged. We spent two years learning to serve it at that exact moment. The kitchen runs like a launch pad: one pass, twelve seats, no shortcuts, and a strict policy that nothing lands before it's ready.

Come watch it hover.
